01

The problem it solves

Most schools already have the information. What they do not have is one place to put it.

Admission forms sit in a filing cabinet. Fee balances live in an exercise book that only the bursar can read. Marks are copied by hand from subject teachers into a class list, then again into report cards. When a parent telephones to ask what their child owes, somebody has to go and look.

This system replaces that with a single record per student. A payment recorded at the bursar's desk immediately clears the balance a parent sees. A mark entered by a subject teacher immediately appears on the report card and in the class position list. An absence marked at morning registration can send the parent an SMS before the first lesson ends.

02

What it covers

Twenty-two modules, grouped here by who actually opens them during a normal school week.

Front office Admissions, the register, and the people in the building

Students

Admission with full biodata, search, editing, promotion between forms, suspension, graduation and archiving. Admission numbers are generated per school.

Parents & guardians

Multiple children per guardian, several guardians per child, with one nominated as the fee payer and one as the primary contact.

Staff

Teaching and non-teaching records, departments, employment terms, staff attendance, leave requests and salary details.

Attendance

Daily class registers, late arrivals and absentees, staff attendance, and automatic SMS to the guardians of absent students.

School setup

School profile, academic years and terms, classes and streams, subjects, departments and grading scales — all configurable per school.

Documents

A register of admission letters, certificates, transcripts and staff contracts, with storage usage tracked per school.

Academics Teaching, examining and reporting

Timetable

Period-by-period timetables per class with clash detection, so a teacher or a room is never double-booked when a slot is saved.

Examinations

All five exam types, marks entry per subject, grading against your own scale, GPA, optional position ranking, report cards and transcripts.

Assignments & homework

Set by the teacher, visible to the student and the parent, with submission recorded against each student.

Reports

Enrolment, attendance, fee collection, outstanding balances, results analysis and staff reports, exported to CSV for Excel.

Finance Money in, money out, and the books that prove it

Fees

Fee structures per class and term across nine categories, invoice generation, receipts, discounts, scholarships and waivers, with balances always derived from the ledger rather than typed in.

Payments

Cash, bank transfer, cheque, mobile money and card. Part payments allocate across invoices oldest first; reversals are recorded, never deleted.

Accounting

Income and expenses, a general ledger, profit and loss, and cash flow — posted automatically from fee receipts and approved payroll.

Payroll

Monthly runs with per-staff allowances, PAYE and NSSF deductions, payslips, and an approve-then-pay workflow so a draft never touches the ledger.

Operations Everything the school owns and moves

Library

Book catalogue and categories, borrowing and returns, overdue tracking and late fees, with copies counted per title.

Inventory

Assets, stationery, laboratory equipment and furniture, with stock movements, suppliers and purchase orders.

Transport

Buses, routes and stops, drivers, fuel logs, maintenance records, student allocation and printable route manifests.

Hostel

Rooms and beds with occupancy tracking, and allocation that respects gender and capacity rules.

Leadership What the head teacher and the board see

Dashboard

Enrolment, staffing, fees collected this month, outstanding balances, today's attendance, upcoming examinations and enrolment by class — on one screen.

Communication

Noticeboard announcements, saved message templates, and bulk SMS or email to a chosen audience.

Users & permissions

Accounts, roles, password resets, session management and automatic lockout after repeated failed sign-ins.

Audit trail

Every change of consequence is recorded with who made it and when — which matters most where money is concerned.

05

Reaching parents

SMS is how Tanzanian schools actually reach families, so it is built in rather than bolted on. Messages send through Africa's Talking; email sends through your own mail account.

  • Write one message and send it to all parents, the parents of one class, all staff, or only the parents with outstanding fees.
  • Each family receives their own version: “Dear {{guardianName}}, the balance for {{studentName}} is TZS {{balance}}” arrives with the real names and the real figure.
  • Absence alerts send automatically when a register is submitted.
  • Every message is logged with its delivery status and the cost the gateway reported, so the school can see exactly what its credit was spent on.
  • A message that fails for a reason that could pass later — a network timeout, an empty balance — is retried. One that never could, such as a wrong number, is not, so credit is not wasted.
SMS credit is bought from Africa's Talking directly by the school. It is not part of the licence fee, so the school controls its own spending and can see the per-message cost in the message log.

Hosted subscription

Recommended for most schools

For schools that want it in-house

07

Security and your data

  • Passwords are never stored. They are hashed with Argon2id, the current standard, so even we cannot read them. Repeated failed sign-ins lock the account temporarily.
  • Sessions expire and can be revoked. Suspending a member of staff signs them out everywhere immediately.
  • Permissions are enforced at the server, not merely hidden in the menus, so a link cannot be shared to reach something a role should not see.
  • On the hosted service, schools are strictly separated. Every record carries its school, and every request is filtered by it. No school can see another's data.
  • Changes are audited. Who did what, and when — particularly around fees, payments and results.
  • Nothing financial is deleted. A wrong receipt is reversed, and both entries remain visible, which is what an auditor expects to find.
  • Encrypted in transit over HTTPS throughout, with daily database backups on the hosted service.

10

Getting started

A typical rollout, timed to begin in the holiday before a new term.

Week 1

Setup and configuration

Your school profile, academic year and terms, classes, streams, subjects and grading scale. At the end of the week the system reflects how your school is actually organised.

Week 2

Loading your records

Students, guardians and staff imported from your existing lists, then fee structures and opening balances entered and reconciled against your books.

Week 3

Training

Separate sessions for the office, the bursar and the teaching staff, each covering only what that group uses. Roughly half a day per group.

Week 4

Parallel running

The first weeks of term are run both ways — on the system and as you do now — so nothing is lost while confidence is built. Parent portal accounts are issued once the balances are confirmed correct.

Term 2 onward

Live

The old books are retired. Support continues under the agreed arrangement.

The next step is a demonstration

The most useful thing we can do is sit with your bursar and one class teacher for an hour and walk through a real week: take a register, enter a set of marks, receipt a fee payment, and send a message to a class. Bring your fee structure and a class list and we will set the demonstration up with your own figures rather than ours.
